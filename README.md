A 1024×1024 editorial front-page infographic that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026 in six verified stories. A full-width lead panel covers Trump's arrival in Manila for Quad talks ahead of a China visit and a meeting with Xi and Putin; a two-column grid in the middle covers Ukraine's second consecutive day of strikes on the Kerch Bridge, Iran's drone attack on a US radar system in Bahrain and reported drone attacks over Kuwait, Canada and China extending trade deals ahead of the APEC summit, and Britain sanctioning Israeli settlement firms; a bottom strip closes the page with MS Dhoni's retirement from the IPL and his new role as Chennai Super Kings mentor. Everything is drawn in pure Pillow shapes and text — no photos, no network calls — in a deep teal, amber and cream palette.

`build_image.py` renders the infographic to `entry.png`, and `main.py` copies that file into the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
